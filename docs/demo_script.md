# 90-Second Demo Script for Judges & Stakeholders

**Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges, Paramedics, Emergency Response Coordinators  
**Total Runtime:** ~90 Seconds

---

### [00:00 - 00:15] The Hook & Problem Statement
*(Speaker looks at the judge or camera)*
> "In acute emergencies, every 60 seconds of delay increases mortality. But information never arrives clean—it comes as panicked voice recordings, blurry prescription photos, crumbled medical records, and heavy rain or road closures. 
> This is **LifeLine**: an emergency intelligence pipeline that converts chaotic multi-modal inputs into structured, verified, prioritized life-saving actions in under 5 seconds."

---

### [00:15 - 00:35] Demo Preset 1: Cardiac Collapse & Pill Strip
*(Action: Click Preset 1 button on UI)*
> "Notice our live 5-stage pipeline stepper: **Ingest**, **Extract**, **Verify**, **Decide**, and **Act**.
> Here, a bystander reports their father collapsed with chest pain, and uploads a photo of a blister pack. 
> LifeLine identifies **Sorbitrate (5mg)**, cross-checks antiplatelet rules, flags an acute **CRITICAL** triage, and provides a **1-Tap Call to 112** alongside a **20-Second Dispatcher Script** that anyone can read aloud without freezing."

---

### [00:35 - 00:55] Demo Preset 2 & 3: Clinical Interactions & Route Intelligence
*(Action: Click Preset 2, then Preset 3)*
> "LifeLine's true differentiator is **Verification**. In Preset 2, a diabetic patient with 3 years of records develops a sudden 103°F fever. LifeLine doesn't just summarize—it checks our clinical dataset and flags a dangerous **Metformin accumulation / Lactic Acidosis risk**, instructing the ER team to hold the drug.
> In Preset 3, a highway accident is matched with smart city weather and traffic data. Notice how LifeLine detects waterlogging on the expressway and routes the ambulance via a clear green corridor to the nearest trauma center."

---

### [00:55 - 01:20] Demo Preset 4: Human-in-the-Loop Clarification (Never Guessing Silently)
*(Action: Click Preset 4)*
> "Watch what happens when inputs contradict each other: a panicked caller shouts 'No allergies!', but an EHR record shows a severe Penicillin allergy.
> Instead of hallucinating or silently guessing, LifeLine flags a **CRITICAL CONFLICT** and triggers a **Human-in-the-Loop Clarification Prompt**, allowing the user to confirm the allergy before generating the paramedic handover."

---

### [01:20 - 01:30] Localization & Export
*(Action: Toggle Hindi/Marathi language buttons, then click 'Export PDF')*
> "With one click, LifeLine supports **English, Hindi, and Marathi**, and generates a standardized, printable **Paramedic ER Handoff Report**. LifeLine is built to save lives when seconds count."
